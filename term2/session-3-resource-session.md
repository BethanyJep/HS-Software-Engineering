# Session 3 – Resource Session

**Slides:** [Open the Resource Session presentation](session-3-resources-slides.html)

**Term:** 2  
**Duration:** 90 minutes  
**Audience:** Grade 10 students, Alliance Girls High School  
**Facilitators:** TOFA / Alumni volunteers (2–3 recommended)  
**Focus:** Hands-on setup, resources, and choosing an individual Term 3 capstone

---

## Session Objectives

By the end of this session, students will be able to:

1. Access at least two free online learning platforms.
2. Access and navigate a beginner coding environment without creating an account.
3. Compare the three Term 3 capstone options.
4. Choose their own capstone and feel ready to begin.

---

## Materials Needed

- [ ] Computer lab or student devices (1 per student where possible)
- [ ] Reliable internet access
- [ ] Projector / display screen
- [ ] Facilitator laptop (demos tested beforehand)
- [ ] "My Starter Kit" handout — one per student (see Appendix A)
- [ ] "Capstone Selection Canvas" — one per student (see Appendix B)
- [ ] Whiteboard / flip chart for idea collection

---

## Session Plan

### ⏱ 0–10 min | Welcome & Icebreaker

**Facilitator action:** Welcome back, quick recap of Sessions 1 & 2.

> 💡 See [Icebreakers](../resources/icebreakers.md). **Recommended:** *Tech Trivia Lightning Round* or *My Superpower in a Team* (gets students thinking about the strengths they bring to a project).

---

### ⏱ 10–30 min | Hands-On: Getting Set Up

**Purpose:** Every student leaves with the tools open and ready to learn.

**Step-by-step with students (facilitator does it on projector, students follow):**

#### Step 1 – Create a Google Account (if needed)
> Most students will have one, but confirm. A Google account unlocks many tools.

#### Step 2 – Set up on Scratch (scratch.mit.edu)
- Go to `scratch.mit.edu`
- Click "Join Scratch" → create a free account
- Open a new project and change the cat's costume (just to confirm it works!)
- Facilitator demos: *"In 2 minutes I'm going to make this cat say hello."*

#### Step 3 – Set up on NitroIDE (nitroide.com) — for more advanced students
- Go to `nitroide.com/tools/codebox.html`
- No account or install is needed.
- In `index.html`, write `<h1>Hello, Alliance Girls!</h1>`
- Run it and celebrate! 🎉

#### Step 4 – Bookmark these learning resources
Have students open and bookmark (or save in a shared doc):

| Platform | URL | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Scratch | scratch.mit.edu | Visual programming, beginners |
| freeCodeCamp | freecodecamp.org | Web development (HTML, CSS, JS) |
| CS50 | cs50.harvard.edu | Comprehensive CS fundamentals (free) |
| Khan Academy Computing | khanacademy.org/computing | Self-paced, great explanations |
| Thunkable | thunkable.com | Build mobile apps, no code needed |
| Figma | figma.com | UI/UX design, free for students |
| GitHub | github.com | Store and share code (introduce later) |
| w3schools | w3schools.com | Quick HTML/CSS/JS reference |

---

### ⏱ 30–50 min | 🔥 Live Demo – Building Something from Scratch

**Purpose:** Give students a taste of what "building" actually feels like.

> 💡 Preview [Term 3 Lesson 1](../term3/lessons/lesson-1-html-landing-page.md):
> - **Build a personal "About Me" webpage** (HTML + CSS, ~15 min)
>   - Students follow along on their own devices
>   - End result: a webpage with their name, a colour they chose, and one sentence about them

**Facilitator talking points as you build:**
- *"This is exactly how every app starts — one small piece at a time."*
- *"Don't worry if yours looks different from mine — that's actually design!"*
- *"This is something you could show to a future employer."*

---

### ⏱ 50–65 min | Choosing a Capstone

**Purpose:** Each student selects one project brief to build on their own in Term 3. There are no
teams.

**Step 1 – Compare the Capstones (10 min)**
Read the [three capstone options](../term3/project-ideas.md). Each student ranks them and thinks
about which problem and career paths interest them most.

**Step 2 – Complete the Capstone Selection Canvas (5 min)**
Each student selects one brief and fills in their own canvas (Appendix B).

---

### ⏱ 65–80 min | Students Share Their Ideas

Invite volunteers to take 1 minute each to share:
1. The capstone they selected
2. Who the solution is for
3. Which career roles they want to try first

Facilitator and peers offer one **"warm"** (positive) and one **"wonder"** (question or suggestion) for each speaker.

---

### ⏱ 80–90 min | Wrap-Up & Looking Ahead to Term 3

**Key messages:**
- The tools are set up — now it's time to start exploring and building.
- Over the holidays / start of Term 3, challenge yourself to complete ONE tutorial on your chosen platform.
- Term 3 will be exciting — you'll have support from facilitators throughout.

**Hand out:** "My Starter Kit" reference card (Appendix A).

**Announce:**
- Project guidelines document will be shared — see [Term 3 Project Guidelines](../term3/project-guidelines.md).
- Term 3 kickoff session date.

## Facilitator Notes

- Ensure the computer lab booking is confirmed well in advance.
- Test all URLs from the school's network before the session — some may be blocked.
- Have an offline backup plan (e.g., Scratch offline editor) in case of poor internet.
- Don't rush capstone selection; every student should understand their chosen brief.
- Collect Capstone Selection Canvases and share them with all facilitators before Term 3 starts.
- If students don't finish their canvas, they can complete it at the start of Term 3.
