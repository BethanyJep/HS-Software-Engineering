01

PAIR TASK · 5 MIN

ICT is already
in your day.

Information and Communication Technology helps us store, process and share information.

  • HardwarePoint to something you can touch.
  • SoftwareName the instructions it runs.
  • ConnectionHow does a message reach a friend?
02

NOTICE · 5 MIN

What could
work better?

Think about school, your neighbourhood or a local club.

  • NoticeWrite one everyday frustration.
  • ShareWho experiences it?
  • ChoosePick one need your group can explore.
03

EMPATHISE + DEFINE · 8 MIN

Listen before
you build.

  • Interview“Tell me about the last time this happened.”
  • ListenWrite the need, not your solution.
  • Define“Our user needs ___ because ___.”

Swap interviewer and storyteller. Use no private details.

04

IDEATE · 8 MIN

AI can suggest.
You decide.

AI learns patterns from examples; its answers can be wrong.

Suggest 3 simple website ideas to help a school club share opportunities. Use free tools. For each idea, name one limitation.

Try this in a school-approved AI tool with your facilitator. No access? Brainstorm as the “human idea engine”.

05

CRITIQUE · 6 MIN

Useful?
Fair? Possible?

  • CheckWhich claim needs a trusted source?
  • IncludeWho might this idea leave out?
  • ProtectNever enter names, passwords or private stories.

Keep one idea. Improve one. Reject one and explain why.

06

CONNECT TO YOUR CAPSTONE · 5 MIN

One community.
Three starting points.

  • Opportunities HubHelp students find their next step. Web + design.
  • Weather DashboardHelp a club plan activities. Data + cloud.
  • CyberSmart QuestHelp peers practise safer choices. Security + games.

Choose a direction from the three capstone briefs. Make its local audience yours.

07

PROTOTYPE · 10 MIN

Draw it.
Make it tangible.

  • A headlineWho does your page help?
  • One useful thingWhat information do they need?
  • One actionWhat should they click next?

One sheet. Three parts. No code yet.

08

TEST + ITERATE · 8 MIN

Let someone
try it.

  • Hand it overAsk a peer to find the next action.
  • Stay quietWatch where they hesitate.
  • RedrawChange one confusing part.

Design thinking: listen → define → ideate → prototype → test → repeat.

09

Leave with
an idea worth testing.

  • Group evidenceThree ideas from your AI or offline brainstorm.
  • A prototypeYour chosen idea and one peer-led improvement.
  • Your reflectionHow did design thinking and technology improve your idea?
