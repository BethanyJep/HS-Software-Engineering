# November–December Holiday Task 🏠

**Audience:** Grade 10 students, Alliance Girls High School  
**Dates:** Monday 2 November – Sunday 20 December 2026 (7 weeks)  
**Deadline:** Submit the [Capstone Showcase Form](https://github.com/BethanyJep/HS-Software-Engineering/issues/new?template=capstone-showcase.yml) by **Sunday 20 December 2026**  
**Time:** Two or three sessions of 45–60 minutes each week  
**Work:** On your own. There are no teams.

> **Your task:** Teach yourself Lessons 5–8 and use them to finish **your own** capstone website.
> Publish it, take screenshots, and submit the showcase form.

Everything you need is linked on this page. Bookmark it, or save it to your phone, and come back
to it at the start of every session.

### Week 1 · 2 – 8 November · Set Up and Add Interactivity

**First session: set up (about 45 minutes)**

- [ ] Read the [Holiday Self-Study Pack](holiday-self-study.md) once from top to bottom.
- [ ] Fill in your [Holiday Capstone Plan](holiday-self-study.md#holiday-capstone-plan) if you have
  not done so already. Re-read your brief:
  [School Opportunities Hub](project-ideas.md#option-1--school-opportunities-hub),
  [Kenya Weather Dashboard](project-ideas.md#option-2--kenya-weather-dashboard), or
  [CyberSmart Quest](project-ideas.md#option-3--cybersmart-quest).
- [ ] Open your capstone in [NitroIDE](https://nitroide.com/tools/codebox.html). If you are on a new
  device, create a new project and paste your saved code back in. The HTML panel only takes what
  goes **inside** `<body>`.
- [ ] Start a learning journal using the
  [journal template](holiday-self-study.md#learning-journal).

**Then: Lesson 5**

- [ ] Work through the [Lesson 5 slides](https://bethanyjep.github.io/HS-Software-Engineering/term3/week-5-interactivity-slides.html) step by step, with the
  [Lesson 5 written guide](lessons/lesson-5-website-interactivity.md) open for extra detail.
- [ ] Add one working interaction to your capstone.
- [ ] Choose **Prefs → Download ZIP** in NitroIDE at the end of every session.

**Done when:** clicking or typing on your page changes what it shows, and you have a journal entry
for Lesson 5.

### Week 2 · 9 – 15 November · Load Data with Fetch

- [ ] Work through the [Lesson 6 slides](https://bethanyjep.github.io/HS-Software-Engineering/term3/week-6-apis-slides.html) and the
  [Lesson 6 written guide](lessons/lesson-6-apis-and-fetch.md).
- [ ] Build the practice weather widget with [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) (free, no key).
- [ ] Add loading, empty, and error messages to your capstone's data.
- [ ] Download ZIP and write your Lesson 6 journal entry.

**Done when:** your capstone shows "Loading…" and then the data, or a clear message, and never
a blank screen.

### Week 3 · 16 – 22 November · Save Your Work on GitHub

- [ ] With a parent or guardian's permission, create a free [GitHub](https://github.com/) account.
- [ ] Install [Visual Studio Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/) and
  [Git](https://git-scm.com/downloads). If you cannot install software, use the browser-only path
  in the lesson.
- [ ] Work through the [Lesson 7 slides](https://bethanyjep.github.io/HS-Software-Engineering/term3/week-7-git-slides.html) and the
  [Lesson 7 written guide](lessons/lesson-7-git-and-github.md).
- [ ] Make at least three commits with clear messages.

**Done when:** your capstone files are in your own public GitHub repository and the history shows
your commits.

### Week 4 · 23 – 29 November · Publish Your Website

- [ ] Work through the [Lesson 8 slides](https://bethanyjep.github.io/HS-Software-Engineering/term3/week-8-deployment-slides.html) and the
  [Lesson 8 written guide](lessons/lesson-8-deployment.md).
- [ ] Turn on [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/quickstart) and open your live link.
- [ ] Test the live site on a phone and on a computer.

**Done when:** someone else can open your website from its link. If you are not allowed to
publish, run it from your folder and note that in your journal.

> ✋ **Mid-holiday check-in (by Sunday 29 November):** post your live link, or a screenshot, and
> answer the [weekly check-in questions](project-guidelines.md#weekly-check-in-template) in the
> program WhatsApp group or Google Classroom.

### Week 5 · 30 November – 6 December · Test and Polish

- [ ] Tick off every item in the
  [Requirements for Every Project](project-ideas.md#requirements-for-every-project).
- [ ] Ask at least **two people** to use your site without your help. Write down what confused them.
- [ ] Fix the biggest problem they found, then commit and push.
- [ ] Check colour contrast with the [WebAIM contrast checker](https://webaim.org/resources/contrastchecker/)
  and try using your page with only the keyboard (**Tab** and **Enter**).
- [ ] Only if your MVP is complete: add one stretch goal from your brief.

**Done when:** every requirement is ticked and you have made one change based on feedback.

### Week 6 · 7 – 13 December · README and Screenshots

- [ ] Add a `README.md` to your repository (Step 7 of the
  [Lesson 8 slides](https://bethanyjep.github.io/HS-Software-Engineering/term3/week-8-deployment-slides.html)). Credit your data, learning resources, and
  any images you used.
- [ ] Take your screenshots using the
  [how-to-screenshot table](../showcase/README.md#how-to-take-a-screenshot):
  - one at **phone width**
  - one at **computer width**
  - *(optional)* your main feature in action
- [ ] Finish your learning journal for Lessons 5–8.
- [ ] Read the [Showcase Form Guidelines](project-guidelines.md#showcase-form-guidelines) and the
  [judging criteria](project-guidelines.md#judging-criteria).

**Done when:** you have everything in
[What to Bring Back](holiday-self-study.md#what-to-bring-back).

### Week 7 · 14 – 20 December · Submit 🎉

- [ ] Read [Before You Fill In the Form](../showcase/README.md#before-you-fill-in-the-form).
- [ ] Check that your screenshots show no surname, phone number, email, passwords, or photos of
  people.
- [ ] Submit the
  **[Capstone Showcase Form](https://github.com/BethanyJep/HS-Software-Engineering/issues/new?template=capstone-showcase.yml)**
  by **Sunday 20 December 2026**.
- [ ] Check the form over the next few days and reply if a facilitator asks you to change
  something.

**Done when:** your form is submitted. Once a facilitator approves it, your project appears in the
[showcase gallery](../showcase/README.md#showcase-gallery).

### After 20 December · Rest

Enjoy the festive season. If you want more, try one project from the optional
[Holiday Build Challenge](../resources/career-paths/holiday-build-challenge.md) or explore a field
in [Career Paths in Tech](../resources/career-paths/README.md). Judges review submissions and
winners are announced when school reopens.

## Rules, Safety, and Help

- **Your own work.** Family and friends may test your site and encourage you, but you write the
  code. You must be able to explain everything you submit. AI tools may explain ideas, but must
  not build the project for you. See the [project rules](project-guidelines.md#rules).
- **30-minute rule.** If you are stuck for 30 minutes, write down what you tried and ask in the
  program WhatsApp group or Google Classroom. More tips are in
  [Getting Help](holiday-self-study.md#getting-help).
- **Stay safe online.** Never share passwords or one-time codes, and keep personal details and
  photos of people out of your project. Read [Staying Safe Online](holiday-self-study.md#staying-safe-online).
- **No laptop or limited internet?** Read
  [No Laptop or Limited Internet?](holiday-self-study.md#no-laptop-or-limited-internet) and tell
  your facilitator early. A smaller MVP that works is fine.
- **Free only.** Nothing in this task needs a credit card, paid plan, or secret API key.

> Finished is better than perfect. A small project that works and that you can explain is a real
> achievement.
